1 · First launch and welcome

One welcome for every member, with no country detection or picker. Four slides cover results, the insurance card, claims and privacy. Language follows the device and can be changed on the first slide.

2 · Sign-up and insurance linking

The current sign-up and OTP, followed by insurance linking. A failed check or an unreachable insurer still lets the member in. Consent (2.7) sits between the form and the code, stored with its wording and date.

3 · Vault

The first tab and the member's dashboard: latest results with reports, banners, the insurance summary and upcoming reminders for medications, tests and appointments. Each block opens its own tab.

4 · Results

The current Test Results screen, with two additions: each lab's logo, and Test reminders. Results arrive automatically from connected labs; members add the rest themselves, and one list shows both.

5 · Insurance

Card, eligibility, policy, Table of Benefits, provider network, claims, pre-approvals, payments and booking, in one tab. Every balance shows when it was last read from the insurer.

6 · Renewing the policy

Renewal opens 30 days before the policy ends. Individual and family policies renew in the app: plan, co-pay, members, then payment. Employer policies are renewed by HR, so those members see status only.

7 · Health

Four tabs: Sensors, Health figures, Medications and Prescriptions. Readings come from Apple Health or Health Connect, each with its source. MedVault doesn't diagnose, and the insurer never sees any of it.

8 · More

Profile, family, settings, language, support and account. Each family member has their own card, results and reminders, and every tab can be filtered by person.

9 · Ask MedVault, the AI assistant

Phase 3
A chat about the member's own cover, claims, results and medicines, opened from Vault and the provider network. Answers cite their sources and suggest a next step. It doesn't diagnose, can be switched off per insurer, and the insurer never reads the chats.

10 · Arabic, right to left

Every screen in English and Arabic, with a full right-to-left layout. Amounts and card numbers keep Western digits by default. Gauges, forms, charts, digits and consent are shown in 10.6 to 10.10; the complete Arabic set follows once the English copy is final.

11 · Phases and dependencies

Each feature mapped to a delivery phase and what it depends on. Until a feature is live, it shows Coming soon. Screens without a badge are Phase 1. The split is proposed for discussion.

Phase 1Digital health foundationOne app for every member, English and Arabic with full RTL. Al Ettihad and iCare connected. Self-service eligibility, benefits and network. Self-pay onboarding, booking, payments, banners.
Phase 2Healthcare network expansionHospitals, labs, radiology and pharmacies integrated. Claims and pre-approvals streamlined. More providers in the network.
Phase 3Intelligent health servicesAI assistance and analytics, telehealth, caregiver tools, medication fulfilment and delivery.

  • Card artwork and branding
  • Whether premiums can be collected in the app

TPA APIiCare

  • Provider network with type, location and billing mode
  • Claim submission with documents, and the document list per claim type

  • Used balances per benefit
Blocks: network, claims, pre-approvals, remaining limits
